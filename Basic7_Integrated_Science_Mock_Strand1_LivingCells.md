### Question 1 (8 marks)

**(a)** [1 mark]

- A cell is the smallest (basic) unit of structure and function of a living thing.
- It is the smallest part of a living thing that can carry out all life processes.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark. Accept any answer that gives the idea of 'smallest / basic unit of a living thing'. Do NOT award a mark for 'a cell is a small thing' or for naming a cell organelle.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- All living things are made up of cells, so the cell is the building block of every organism.
- The cell is the smallest part of an organism that can carry out all the life processes (feeding, growth, respiration, reproduction).
- Nothing smaller than a cell can live and work on its own.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Do NOT award a mark for simply repeating 'it is the basic unit of life' without explanation.*

**(c)** [3 marks]

- Peel a thin, transparent piece of skin from an onion leaf.
- Put it flat on a glass slide, add a drop of water (and a stain such as iodine solution), and cover it with a cover slip.
- Place the slide on the stage of the microscope and focus, first with the low-power lens and then with the high-power lens.
- Observe that the onion skin is made up of many small, box-like compartments, showing that the plant is made up of cells.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for preparing a thin specimen on a slide. 1 mark for using the microscope correctly (slide on the stage, focus with the low-power lens first). 1 mark for stating the observation - many small box-like compartments - leading to the conclusion that the plant is made of cells. Award method marks even if the conclusion is poorly worded.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- Cells are extremely small - thousands of them can fit on a pinhead.
- The human eye cannot magnify, so it cannot make objects that small large enough to be seen.
- A microscope magnifies the cells so that they become visible.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Accept 'they are too small' developed with the idea of magnification. Do NOT award both marks for the same point said twice.*

  - *Marking note: 1 mark for naming chlorophyll. 1 mark for stating that it traps/absorbs light energy for making food. Accept 'it makes the plant green' only as part of a correct answer, not on its own.*

**(b)** [3 marks]

- It will grow poorly, become weak and yellow, and may eventually die.
