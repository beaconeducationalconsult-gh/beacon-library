## 7. CURRICULUM COVERAGE WITHIN PAPERS

### 7.1 Topic Coverage per Paper

Each BECE Mock paper focuses on a single content standard/sub-strand, with all 40 MCQs and 6 theory questions drawn from that topic. This provides **deep coverage** of each area.

**Example: B7 Living Cells**
- 40 MCQs covering: cells as units of life (6), animal cell structure (8), organelle functions (7), plant cell structure (7), plant organelle functions (6), comparing cells (6)
- 6 theory questions covering: labelling diagrams, functions, comparison, drawing, modelling, application

**Assessment:** The single-topic-per-paper approach provides **excellent depth** but means each paper tests only one content standard. This is appropriate for end-of-topic tests but not for terminal examinations that should cover the full curriculum.

**Recommendation:** For the final publication, clearly position:
- Individual topic papers as **end-of-topic tests** or **revision practice**
- The Beacon Mock Packs (which compile 20 papers) as **terminal examination preparation**
- The Special Mock (full curriculum) as **comprehensive revision**

## 9. ISSUES AND RECOMMENDATIONS

### 9.1 Issues Found

| # | Issue | Severity | Location | Recommendation |
|---|-------|:--------:|----------|----------------|
| 1 | MCQ sections are recall-heavy (72% R+U) | 🟡 MEDIUM | All papers | Replace 10–15% of Remember items with Apply/Analyse |
| 2 | Some papers have 0 Evaluate/Create MCQs | 🟡 MEDIUM | Energy, Force & Motion, Life Cycle, Ecosystem | Add 1–2 higher-order MCQs per paper |
| 3 | Difficulty claim (30/50/20) not matched by data | 🟡 MEDIUM | Beacon Pack front matter | Revise claim or adjust question difficulty |
| 4 | Answer distribution is mechanically balanced | 🟢 LOW | 19 of 20 B7 papers | Allow natural variation (9–11 per letter) |
| 5 | Water Cycle Q1 identical in B and C | 🟢 LOW | Collections B and C | Acceptable — generic question independently written |
| 6 | Collection D = Collection B (by design) | ℹ️ NOTE | All B7/B8/B9 packs | Document this relationship clearly |

### 9.2 Recommendations for Publication

1. **Adjust cognitive demand** — For each paper, replace 4–6 Remember-level MCQs with Apply or Analyse items to bring the distribution closer to BECE standards.

2. **Revise difficulty claim** — Change the Beacon Pack statement from "30% easy, 50% moderate, 20% challenging" to "approximately 60% easy, 28% moderate, 12% challenging" or adjust the questions to match the claim.

3. **Position papers clearly** — In the final books, clearly state:
   - Topic papers = end-of-topic tests
   - Compiled packs = terminal examination preparation
   - Special Mock = comprehensive revision

4. **Add higher-order MCQs** — Ensure every paper has at least:
   - 5 Apply-level items
   - 3 Analyse-level items
   - 1 Evaluate or Create item

5. **Document the collection relationships** — In the front matter of each book, explain:
   - Collection D is the compiled edition of Collection B
   - Collection C provides additional practice on the same topics
   - All three can be used together for comprehensive revision
