SECTION B: THEORY — MARKING SCHEME
General Marking Notes: Award method marks (M) for correct approach. Award accuracy marks (A) for correct final answer. Accept reasonable alternative answers. Do not penalise spelling errors unless meaning is changed.

Question 1 [Total: 16 marks]

(a) Define the term agricultural tool. [2 marks]
An agricultural tool is any instrument or device [1M] used by farmers to carry out farming activities [1M].
Alternative: An agricultural tool is a device used on the farm to make farm work easier. [Accept]
(b) State four differences between simple and mechanical agricultural tools. [8 marks]
Simple Tools Mechanical Tools
Operated by hand [1M] Powered by engine or motor [1M]
Cheaper to buy [1M] More expensive [1M]
Suitable for small farms [1M] Suitable for large farms [1M]
Slower in operation [1M] Faster in operation [1M]
Other acceptable differences: requires less skill / requires more skill; lower maintenance / higher maintenance. [Award 1M for each valid difference, max 8 marks]
