SECTION B: THEORY — MARKING SCHEME

Question 1 [Total: 16 marks]

Marking Notes: Award method marks (M) for correct approach. Award accuracy marks (A) for correct final answer. Accept reasonable alternative answers. Do not penalise spelling errors unless meaning is changed.

(a) Define the term agricultural tool. [2 marks]

An agricultural tool is any instrument or device [1M] used by farmers to carry out farming activities [1M].
Alternative: An agricultural tool is a device used on the farm to make farm work easier. [Accept]

(b) State four differences between simple and mechanical agricultural tools. [8 marks]

Simple Tools Mechanical Tools
Operated by hand [1M] Powered by engine or motor [1M]
Cheaper to buy [1M] More expensive [1M]
Suitable for small farms [1M] Suitable for large farms [1M]
Slower in operation [1M] Faster in operation [1M]
Other acceptable differences: requires less skill / requires more skill; less labour-intensive / more labour-intensive; lower maintenance / higher maintenance. [Award 1M for each valid difference, max 8 marks]

Tool Use

Section B: Theory

Question 4

(a) Write the balanced chemical equation for the reaction between hydrogen and oxygen to form water. [3 marks]
2H2 + O2 → 2H2O
[1M for correct reactants, 1M for correct products, 1A for correct balancing]
(b) Calculate the volume of CO2 produced when 50 cm³ of methane (CH4) burns completely in oxygen. [4 marks]

CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O [1M for equation]

From the equation: 1 volume of CH4 produces 1 volume of CO2 [1M]

Therefore: 50 cm³ of CH4 produces 50 cm³ of CO2 [1A]

Volume of CO2 = 50 cm³ [1A]
