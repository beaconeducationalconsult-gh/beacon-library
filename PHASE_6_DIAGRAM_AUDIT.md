## 6. DIAGRAM PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

### 6.1 Sample Production Specification — Animal Cell

**Diagram ID:** A-02-Fig1
**Book:** B7 Integrated Science
**Chapter/Topic:** Living Cells
**Question:** Theory Q1–Q3
**Purpose:** Labelled animal cell for identification and function questions
**Diagram Type:** Scientific illustration (cross-section)
**Required Labels:** Cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondrion, vacuole, ribosome
**Labels to Omit:** Cell wall, chloroplast (these are plant cell features)
**Arrow Requirements:** Leader lines from each label to the corresponding structure
**Orientation:** Landscape (2:1 ratio)
**Approximate Aspect Ratio:** 2.0:1
**Expected Print Size:** 120 mm × 60 mm (fits within A4 text width)
**Line Weight:** 1.5 pt for cell outline; 1 pt for internal structures
**Font:** Sans-serif (Arial or Helvetica); 8–10 pt for labels
**Shading:** Light grey fill for cytoplasm; no shading for other structures
**Colour Requirement:** Black and white sufficient; optional light blue for cytoplasm
**Black-and-white suitability:** ✅ Excellent
**Caption:** Fig. 1: A labelled animal cell showing the main organelles
**Student Interaction:** Students name the labelled parts and state their functions
**Special Instructions:** Nucleus should be drawn as a large rounded structure near the centre; mitochondria as sausage-shaped with internal folds; cell membrane as a thin irregular outline

**Purpose:** Show the four main stages of the water cycle and how they connect
**Diagram Type:** Conceptual flow diagram with landscape context
**Required Labels:** Evaporation, Condensation, Precipitation, Transpiration, Collection, Sun, Clouds, Ocean/River, Plants
**Labels to Omit:** None — all stages must be shown
